Questions buyers ask

What does TENSPACE supply?

TENSPACE supplies phone replacement batteries, GaN and fast chargers, power banks, charging cables, wired and wireless earphones, tempered glass and phone holders. Buyers are wholesalers, distributors, repair shops and online sellers. You can mix several categories in one order and ship them together from Guangzhou.

Is TENSPACE a factory or a trading company?

TENSPACE sells factory-direct, without a middleman, from 59 Yanjiang West Road in Guangzhou’s Liwan district, next to the New Asia mobile accessories market. Buyers are welcome to inspect production on site or by video call before placing an order.

Can I start with a small trial order?

Yes. Minimum order quantities are flexible, so you can test a few models in small quantities before committing to a larger order. Tell us the models and quantities you need and we will confirm what is in stock and what needs production.

How long does an OEM order with my logo take?

Orders with your logo printed on the product and your own packaging take about 30 days. Standard models without customisation ship faster. Lead time depends on model and quantity, so we confirm the date in writing with your quote.

Which certificates do your products have?

Our batteries carry CE, RoHS and UN38.3 certification. UN38.3 test reports are what freight forwarders and airlines ask for when you ship lithium batteries and power banks. Ask for the certificate files for the exact models you plan to order.

How do you check quality before shipping?

Battery parts are fully inspected and every batch is checked before it leaves Guangzhou. Our battery range uses A-grade cells. Each order is traceable, so if a problem appears later we can identify the batch it came from.

What happens if goods arrive faulty?
